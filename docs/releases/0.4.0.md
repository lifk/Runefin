Runefin 0.4.0 is **Nocturne**: a quieter, denser big-screen interface built around artwork,
type and one focus model.

**A 76 pt rail and a Libraries hub.** Five anchors — Home, Search, Libraries, Live TV, Settings —
in a rail that shows icons at rest and expands to 284 pt as an overlay when the reader enters it,
so the page never shifts. Libraries is a hub one level down: the last three libraries as large
Recent tiles, every library below, a filter, Tools (Downloads, Remote, Home rails, Controls),
Watchlist, Seerr and playlists. Back from a library returns to the hub with its tile focused.

**Home.** A 50 % billboard for the first Continue watching entry, a combined "Recently added"
rail with its See-all shelf, the library chip strip, and up to four pinned library rails. Pins
are per device and chosen from Home or Settings → Home rails. Six rails by default, nine with
four pins.

**Library.** Six columns with 48 pt gutters and the A–Z letters in the right gutter; the toolbar
keeps its sorts on one line and its filters on the next; a 10,000-item library still measures six
columns because grid and list are virtualized.

**Detail.** The season strip scrolls and reports its total; episodes page ten at a time with
Previous/Next and an n–m line; a person shows the first 36 credits and Show all; the chapter
sheet lists all 300 chapters with an OSD transition.

**Live and utility screens.** The guide virtualizes channel rows and pins a preview with the
focused channel, its programme and Watch; an idle Remote session shows Play/Stop; a non-empty
playlist shows Play all, Shuffle and Edit; Settings is a section list beside a scrolling detail
pane.

**Motion and focus.** Page changes are directional (a push rises, Back settles from above), a
card opens into its detail through a matched flight, cards and rail items scale on focus, and
focus is a 2 pt edge inside the card or control. The old white halo behind a card is a dark lift
shadow, so a clipped rail can no longer cut a pale line out of the artwork.

**D-pad.** Every screen's zones follow the page as drawn. A rail's See all is its header row,
Left leaves content for the rail, Settings walks its rows with the section list on the left, and
the library grid no longer stops at the last mounted row. A new test presses every arrow from
every visible control on Home, hub, library, Settings, guide, detail, person, downloads and remote
and fails if focus moves against the press.

**Known limits.** The OSD keeps its previous composition; Settings is one scrolling pane beside
the section list rather than two independent panes; playback and the OSD still need a real display
to verify. The 40-library hub, 10k library, 60-channel guide, 20-season series, 100-credit person
and 300-chapter sheet are unit-tested rather than captured from the review server, which has 8
libraries, 354 movies and 4 channels.

`scripts/check.sh` is green: 502 app tests (27 ignored), workspace clippy, guardrails and the
release build. CI builds and signs the Flatpak.
