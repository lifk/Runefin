# Modern TV media UI research — 2026-11-26

Research for the Runefin redesign. Three parallel reviews: current 10-foot media UIs, the Rune
toolkit's real capabilities, and the current Runefin screens. Sources are at the bottom.

---

## 1. What the good ones do

- **Neutral chrome, artwork carries the colour.** Netflix keeps red below ~5 % of pixels; Max,
  Apple TV and Plex use near-black/graphite surfaces and let posters and backdrops supply hue.
  Blue (or any accent) is reserved for focus, progress and one primary action.
- **Five or six destinations, not fifteen.** Netflix and Disney+ moved to top tabs; Max, Prime
  Video and Plex use a persistent left rail. Either works when Back restores nav focus and the
  rail persists while content scrolls.
- **A 60 % billboard, not 90 %.** Hero is 55–65 % of the viewport height, 16:9 key art, logo
  bottom-left, Resume + one secondary action, a bottom/left scrim, and the next rail peeking
  above the fold.
- **Density from card count, not smaller type.** At 1080p logical: 16:9 rails ~270×152 with six
  visible, poster grids 190–200 px wide with seven or eight columns, 56–64 px side margins,
  16–20 px gutters, 32–40 px between rails.
- **Mixed ratios with a rule.** 16:9 for Continue Watching/Next Up/episodes, 2:3 for library
  posters, 1:1 for cast/channels; never mixed inside one rail.
- **Focus = scale + lift + brightness.** 1.05–1.10× scale, a layered shadow, +8–12 % brightness
  and a 2 px light edge in 120–180 ms. The focused card can expand into a wide tile with a
  synopsis and two actions.
- **Artwork-derived background.** Sample the focused item's dominant hue; wash the page at
  6–12 %. Keep the chrome neutral so the poster stays true.
- **Clearlogo over repeated titles.** Prefer the server Logo PNG with a gradient scrim.
- **One metadata line.** `2023 · 12 · 92 min · 4K HDR · IMDb 5.4` beats six coloured pills.
- **A minimal player OSD.** Auto-hide, bottom gradient only, chapter ticks and trick-play
  thumbs, dim the video while visible; Up opens chapters/tracks.
- **A D-pad contract.** Vertical moves between rows, horizontal inside a row, one press between
  groups, Back one level then nav, focus never disappears and is restored on return.

## 2. Anti-patterns in older 10-foot UIs

- Accent inflation: blue chips, pills, rules, borders and icons at equal saturation.
- Desktop information architecture in a TV shell: long sidebars, hover assumptions, small type.
- A fixed 1200–1400 px content column with 500 px dead on the right.
- Cards inside cards, outlined sections, coloured panel backgrounds.
- A 2 px hue-only focus ring that disappears over artwork at three metres.
- Pill-shaped metadata and a wall of equal-weight filter chips.
- A hero that eats the whole fold.
- Blank grids and infinite spinners instead of skeleton rails with placeholders.

## 3. Three directions

### A. Nocturne — graphite cinema (recommended base)
Base `#0A0C0F`, raised `#12161B`, text `#F4F6F8`, muted `#8C959D`; Jellyfin cyan only on
focus/progress/primary; 6–12 % artwork hue in the background. Five-item collapsed left rail
(Home, Libraries, Search, Live TV, Profile). 16:9 Continue Watching, 2:3 poster grids, 8 px
radius, no chips. **Pros:** safest with mixed Jellyfin art, most space-efficient. **Cons:**
needs strong type to avoid looking like Plex/Netflix.

### B. Marquee — editorial
Near-black `#0B0B0C`, warm white `#F3EEE6`, muted `#A69F94`, one brass `#D8B26A`; top tab row;
2-up large features plus a 7–8 column poster grid; oversized 32–40 px section heads. **Pros:**
strongest identity, full 16:9 use. **Cons:** Back discipline; hero can overpower library users;
warm accent fights some art.

### C. Big Picture — console
Charcoal + translucent white 4–8 %, electric cyan used as light (glow/underline/progress), never
as a fill. PS5/Steam-style icon row; 5–6 wide 16:9 tiles; focused tile expands into a hero.
**Pros:** best Steam Big Picture fit, highest focus clarity. **Cons:** fewer titles per screen,
poor posters exposed, more motion cost.

**Direction:** Nocturne as the base, Big Picture's focus physics, Marquee's typography and hero
discipline. Move the server's many libraries behind a **Libraries hub** so the rail stays at five
destinations.

## 4. Rune constraints and opportunities

Rune can do: gradients (linear/radial, ≤16 stops, Oklab springs), layered shadows, continuous
corners, `clip_fade`, `clip_soft`, blur subtrees, **backdrop blur on Linux** (4 per frame), tint
fallbacks for glass, image fit/fill/tint, variable-font type, spring motion, focus rings/fills,
virtualized rows, safe area, HDR (scRGB), and real point lights in `rune-light`.

It cannot do: arbitrary CSS/SVG documents, conic/repeating gradients, arbitrary masks, image
brightness/saturation filters, text shadows, CSS-grid templates, scroll-linked animation
primitives. "Darken art under text" needs an overlay; a focus halo outside a scroll clip needs
the current four-bar workaround.

The modern Rune look already exists in `demo/opencode/*`: neutral near-black, 8 pt radii,
hairlines at 6–10 % white, grey selection, one accent. Runefin's own theme does the opposite:
`theme::colors()` mixes `oklch(0.66 0.17 256)` into `bg` (2.8 %), `card` (3.8 %) and
`separator` (4 %), so the field is navy; then accent fills the selected nav pill, progress bars,
chips and Play buttons. Three blue surfaces sit inside 200 px on the library page.

Type today is 32/20/18/14/16/14/14/12/15 with fixed leading; big screen lifts the small tokens
to 16, which collapses card title, label, caption and meta into one size. Geometry: 176×264
posters, 40/64 gutters, 224 sidebar; Home rails stay fixed so a four-poster rail ends two-thirds
across at 1920. Focus uses a 2 pt white ring, a 10 % fill and a four-bar blurred halo at once.

## 5. Current-screen audit (from the live tour)

- Home: sparse rails with large right bands; dimmed cached still as the backdrop; optional
  180 pt hero; placeholders where art is missing.
- Library: four columns at 4K with the A-Z rail and a card-wide empty band; 12 equal filter
  chips in two rows; the last column used to clip before the viewport fix.
- Detail: good two-axis scrim, but no backdrop art in the snapshot and a left column with a
  large empty right; cast and related rails clip at the edge (carousel affordance).
- Search: full-width input, empty right/bottom.
- Live TV: narrow channel list, Watch buttons at the far right, large dead middle.
- Remote: narrow session list; Pause on Idle rows.
- Settings: not yet audited in the live tour.
- Focus: the sidebar ring was six physical pixels at 4K; now a wash. Card focus is ring + fill
  + halo.
